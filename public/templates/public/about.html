About Al Baraq Elect. Ware Tr.

Established in 2012, Al Baraq Elect. Ware Tr. has grown into a reliable electrical materials trading and supply company serving customers throughout the United Arab Emirates.

From our beginning, our focus has been simple: to provide quality electrical materials from trusted brands, offer competitive solutions, and build lasting relationships with our customers and suppliers.

Serving Customers Across the UAE

Today, we serve a broad range of customers across the electrical, construction, MEP, industrial, and oil & gas sectors.

We maintain strong relationships across distributors and manufacturers to ensure efficient sourcing, competitive solutions, and reliable delivery.
